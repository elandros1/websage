Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how WebSage ("WebSage", "we", "us") handles information through this website (https://elandros1.github.io/websage/) and the WebSage Chrome extension. Our approach is simple: we collect as little as possible, and we only process webpage content when you actively ask the extension to do something with it.

Information We Collect

  • Page content you choose to process. When you use a WebSage feature — summarize, extract, compare, or generate a report — the content of the specific page(s) you select is processed in order to produce that result.
  • Your saved research projects. If you save a research project, the pages, summaries, and notes you've chosen to keep are stored so you can return to them later.
  • Contact information you provide. If you email us or otherwise contact us, we keep the information you send in order to respond.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect or track your general browsing history.
  • We do not read, scan, or process pages you have not actively asked WebSage to work with.
  • We do not collect unnecessary personal information such as your name, contacts, or location.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell personal data to third parties.

How We Use Page Content

Page content is processed only to generate the output you asked for — a summary, an extracted fact, a comparison, or a report. Content is used for the purpose of that request and is not repurposed for advertising or unrelated profiling.

Generating a result may involve sending the relevant page content to a third-party AI processing service solely to produce that output. We choose providers that do not use submitted content to train their own models, and we will name our processing provider(s) here once finalized.

Chrome Extension Permissions

WebSage requests only the browser permissions it needs to function — for example, access to the currently active tab when you invoke a feature, and local storage to save your research projects on your device. WebSage does not request permission to read every page you visit in the background.

Data Storage & Retention

Saved research projects are stored locally in your browser via the Chrome extension storage APIs. Where processing requires sending content to a server to generate a result, that content is used only to produce the response and is not retained longer than necessary to do so.

No Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or page content to third parties, under any circumstances.

Children's Privacy

WebSage is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your Choices

You can delete saved research projects at any time from within the extension. Uninstalling the WebSage extension removes its locally stored data from your browser.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as WebSage evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@elandros1.github.io.
