The problem

Research shouldn't mean forty tabs and a blank document.

Every research task online turns into the same slog — and it costs you time you meant to spend thinking, not scrolling.

Too many tabs

Context gets scattered

Ten open tabs, ten different framings of the same topic, and no easy way to hold them all in view at once.

Nothing carries over

Facts get lost between pages

A number you needed from page three is gone by the time you're reading page seven. You end up re-reading to find it again.

Manual synthesis

Writing it up takes hours

Turning scattered notes and half-remembered quotes into something coherent is the slowest part of any research task.

Features

Everything you need to research a topic, in one extension.

WebSage works on the page you're already reading — no copy-pasting into another tool.

Summarize webpages

Get a clear, structured summary of any article, paper, or documentation page in seconds — not a wall of text, an actual outline.

Extract key information

Pull out the facts, figures, quotes, and definitions that matter, without re-reading the whole page to find them again.

Compare multiple webpages

Line up several sources side by side and see at a glance where they agree, disagree, or add something new.

Generate research reports

Turn what you've gathered across pages into one organized report, ready to review, edit, or share.

Save research projects

Group related pages into a project and pick up exactly where you left off, days or weeks later.
