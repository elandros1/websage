3. Information We Collect

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary to provide and improve WebSage.

3.1 Information You Provide

If you contact us by email, we may receive and retain the information included in your message, such as your email address, name if provided, the contents of your message, attachments, and any other information you voluntarily provide.

WebSage does not currently require users to create an account on this website.

3.2 Webpage Content You Ask WebSage to Process

When you actively request a WebSage feature, such as summarizing a webpage, extracting information, comparing sources, or generating a research report, the relevant webpage content may be processed in order to provide the requested feature.

WebSage is designed not to intentionally collect or process unrelated webpages that you have not asked WebSage to work with.

3.3 Saved Research Information

If the WebSage extension provides local research-project storage, information such as saved page references, summaries, notes, and related research information may be stored locally in your browser.

Information stored locally by the extension remains under your browser's local storage environment unless you explicitly use a feature that sends information to a server for processing.

3.4 Technical Information

When you access the website, standard technical information may be processed by the hosting infrastructure, such as IP address, browser type, operating system, request time, and technical error information.

This information may be used for basic website operation, security, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention.

4. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

  • We do not intentionally collect your contacts, precise location, or unrelated personal information.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your information for targeted advertising.
