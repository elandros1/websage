Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how WebSage handles information through the WebSage website and Chrome extension.

WebSage is designed to collect and process only information necessary to provide its features.

2. Information We Process

  • Webpage content you choose to process. When you actively request a summary, extraction, comparison, or research report, the relevant webpage content may be processed to provide that feature.
  • Research information. Information associated with research projects may be stored when you choose to save it.
  • Contact information. If you contact us by email, we receive the information that you voluntarily provide in your message.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

  • We do not intentionally collect your general browsing history.
  • We do not intentionally process webpages that you have not actively asked WebSage to process.
  • We do not intentionally collect your contacts or unrelated personal information.
  • We do not sell personal information to third parties.
  • We do not use webpage content for advertising purposes.

4. How Webpage Content Is Used

Webpage content is processed to provide the WebSage feature that you requested, such as summarization, information extraction, comparison, or report generation.

Depending on the implementation of a particular feature, webpage content may be transmitted to a third-party AI processing provider for the purpose of generating the requested result.

We will update this policy if the data processing practices or service providers materially change.

5. Chrome Extension Permissions

WebSage is designed to request only the permissions reasonably necessary for its features.

The extension may access the active webpage when you explicitly invoke a WebSage feature that requires webpage content.

WebSage is not designed to continuously monitor your browsing activity in the background.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Certain research information may be stored locally in your browser using browser storage mechanisms.

Information transmitted to a processing service may be retained only as necessary to provide the requested functionality, subject to the policies and technical practices of the relevant service provider.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Information may be processed by technical service providers when necessary to operate WebSage, such as AI processing, hosting, security, or infrastructure providers.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through WebSage. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

WebSage is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Your Choices

You may stop using WebSage at any time.

You may also uninstall the Chrome extension to stop its access to webpages and remove locally stored extension data, subject to the behavior of your browser and any remaining copies of data that you separately saved or exported.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as WebSage develops. The "Last updated" date will be changed when material changes are made.
